Brand stance

Trust first. Proven in the lab, felt in the story.

Every piece answers one question for a 40–60 year old: why believe this one is different from every supplement I've bought on trust? Primary persona: MP5 The Protector. Secondary: MP2 Busy 40s. Supporting: the high performer.

Rule 1

Clinical in substance

Every statement traces to a study, a named scientist or a certificate. Real labs, real data, credentials on screen. No hype words.

Rule 2

Emotional in delivery

Science pieces open on a human stake: what changes in your 40s, who depends on you. Every human story carries one proof point.

Rule 3

One look

Tech studio, labs, clean type and data overlays, carried by cinematic grade, music and faces. No whiteboards, stock wellness or influencer gloss.

Production model

Hero films are world-class cinema, not content.

Weekly, repeatable series go to one videographer who also edits. The documentary, The Everyday System, the trial films and the seed film go to a cinema-standard production team and a world-class editor. The bar is UVU-level athlete films and feature documentaries.

Always-on tier

One videographer-editor

Road to London, SAB podcast, founder education. Every Sunday, two edit days a week, plus the studio days.
